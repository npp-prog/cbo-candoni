CBO PATCH 13 — ACCOUNTABLE FORMS AND RAAF
=========================================
Municipal Government of Candoni · 28 September 2026

Phase 1 of bringing the MTO systems into CBO. Nothing existing was rewritten:
every file below is either new, or had lines added to it.


WHAT TO DO
----------

1. Drag the contents of this folder into your cbo-candoni folder and let
   Windows replace the files when it asks. Nothing needs deleting this time.

2. Open a terminal in the project folder and run, in order:

       npm --prefix functions run build
       npx tsc -b
       npm test

   All three must be silent or say "passed". If any of them complains, stop and
   send me what it said.

3. Deploy the rules and indexes first. The indexes take a few minutes to build
   and the screens will show a failed-precondition error until they are ready:

       firebase deploy --only firestore:rules,firestore:indexes

4. Deploy the five new functions:

       firebase deploy --only functions:recordFormMovement,functions:voidFormMovement,functions:prepareRaaf,functions:certifyRaaf,functions:cancelRaaf

5. Push to GitHub. Netlify rebuilds the site by itself.

6. Grant the new functions their Cloud Run invoker, the same way as before.
   They are brand new services, so they will not have it:

       $P = gcloud projects describe cbo-candoni-dev --format="value(projectNumber)"
       gcloud run services add-iam-policy-binding recordformmovement --region=asia-southeast1 --member=allUsers --role=roles/run.invoker --project=cbo-candoni-dev
       gcloud run services add-iam-policy-binding voidformmovement   --region=asia-southeast1 --member=allUsers --role=roles/run.invoker --project=cbo-candoni-dev
       gcloud run services add-iam-policy-binding preparerraaf       --region=asia-southeast1 --member=allUsers --role=roles/run.invoker --project=cbo-candoni-dev
       gcloud run services add-iam-policy-binding certifyraaf        --region=asia-southeast1 --member=allUsers --role=roles/run.invoker --project=cbo-candoni-dev
       gcloud run services add-iam-policy-binding cancelraaf         --region=asia-southeast1 --member=allUsers --role=roles/run.invoker --project=cbo-candoni-dev

   (Check the service names in the Cloud Run console if one is refused —
   Firebase lowercases them and occasionally shortens a long one.)


THEN, BEFORE ANYTHING WILL WORK
-------------------------------

Open  Master Data › Accountable Forms  and add the seven forms the office uses.
Nothing can be received or issued until they exist. The MTO system's own list:

  Code     Name                                          Printed as             Booklet  Serial
  AF51     Accountable Form No. 51 — Official Receipt    ACCT. FORM NO. 51      50       10
  AF52     Accountable Form No. 52                       ACCT. FORM NO. 52      50       10
  AF53     Accountable Form No. 53                       ACCT. FORM NO. 53      50       10
  AF54     Accountable Form No. 54                       ACCT. FORM NO. 54      50       10
  AF56     Accountable Form No. 56 — Cash Ticket         ACCT. FORM NO. 56      50       10
  AF58     Accountable Form No. 58                       ACCT. FORM NO. 58      50       10
  BIR0016  BIR Form No. 0016                             BIR FORM NO. 0016      50       10

Give AF56 a face value if the office issues cash tickets of a fixed amount;
leave it blank for everything else.


HOW TO TRY IT
-------------

  1. Treasury › Accountable Forms › Record movement
     Movement: "Received into stock". Form: AF51. Serials 0007705351 to
     0007705500. Reference: whatever the RIS says. → 150 forms in stock.

  2. Record movement again. Movement: "Issued to an officer". Same form,
     serials 0007705351 to 0007705400, and pick the collecting officer.
     → the card at the top now shows 50 with her and 100 in stock.

  3. Try to issue 0007705380 to 0007705420 to somebody else. It will refuse and
     name the serials that are already in her hands. That refusal is the
     module.

  4. Treasury › RAAF › Prepare RAAF. Pick her, "Collecting officer", and the
     month. The report builds itself from the ledger and from the receipts
     encoded in Collections.

  5. If she has encoded receipts out of serials she was never issued, the
     report says so and cannot be certified until a movement explains it.
